OUR STORY · ប្រវត្តិ

Five man spirit coffee lovers,
one Khmer dream

Finz Coffee began with Five man spirit ,a young Cambodians who wanted a coffee brand that truly belonged to Khmer people. That idea became real in 2017 with a small but striking first shop — Finz Coffee AUB.

Built on fast service, fair prices and genuinely good products, Tube Coffee and Tube Coffee+ have grown into one of Cambodia's most-loved coffee and food chains — now 43 stores strong.

2016

The company is founded

Registered with the Ministry of Commerce as Tube Café Co., Ltd.

2017

First shop opens

Tube Coffee Kdan Two — soft open in March, grand open in August.

2021

Tube Coffee+ arrives

The first Tube Coffee+ opens at Samai Square in January.

Today

43 stores nationwide

8 of them are Tube Coffee+, spanning Phnom Penh and 6 provinces.
